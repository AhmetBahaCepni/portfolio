so_long

A compact 2D maze game about collecting every item and finding the exit.

C2D graphicsMiniLibX

The idea

so_long is a small two-dimensional maze game written in C. The player moves through a map, gathers the collectibles, and reaches the exit.

Under the hood

The project uses MiniLibX to draw the map and its characters. Different layouts turn the same simple rules into new routes to solve.
